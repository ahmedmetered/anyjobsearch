# Candidate profile template

Copy this into the candidate's data folder as `profile.md` and fill it by
interviewing the candidate or importing their documents. Every claim should
have evidence attached; evidence is what makes tailored applications honest.

```markdown
# Candidate profile

## Identity
Name, city/timezone, contact channels the candidate wants used.

## Evidence
Portfolio URL, repositories (with what each proves), publications, live
products, certifications. For each: one line on what it demonstrates.

## Experience
Reverse-chronological. For each role: employer, dates, the work in plain
sentences, and the transferable skill it proves. Function matters more than
title.

## Skills
Primary (can be tested on today), secondary (productive with ramp-up),
learning. No aspirational entries.

## Languages
Each language with the candidate's own declared level and one line on how
they self-assessed it.

## Target
Titles and variants to search for. Markets and remote policy (anywhere / EU /
specific country / onsite-only). Salary range and currency. Deal-breakers.

## Constraints
Work-authorization status and timing gates (permit class, hour caps, start
dates). Notice periods. Anything a recruiter must plan around.

## Behavioral
Work environments that bring out the best (and worst) results. Management
style that works. Autonomy level. Team size preferences.

## Direction
Where the candidate is heading in 2-3 years, and the kinds of tasks that
energize or drain them. Used for the career-alignment dimension.
```

## Import notes

- From an existing CV: keep every fact, cut every adjective. The method needs
  claims plus evidence, not marketing.
- From a LinkedIn export or profile read: treat titles and dates as candidate-
  confirmed, descriptions as raw material to rewrite in the candidate's voice.
- From a portfolio: capture what each artifact proves, not what it is. A
  deployed product proves shipping; a test suite proves verification habits;
  an open-source tool proves the candidate can design for strangers.
